# Plant photos used by the simulated cameras

In SIM mode (`robot_tools/farm_sim.py`) the robot cameras show crops of these real photographs
instead of rendered frames. All images come from Wikimedia Commons, were resized to 1024 px
wide, and keep their original licences (the CC BY-SA images remain under CC BY-SA).
